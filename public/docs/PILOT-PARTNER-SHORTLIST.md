# CoreSense Malaysian Pilot Partner Shortlist

**Research date:** 29 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify organizations that could host, validate, protect participants in, or enable a Malaysian worker heat-risk field pilot.  
**Important:** The ranking below is a CoreSense prioritization based on published capabilities and collaboration pathways. It does not indicate that any organization has agreed to participate or endorsed CoreSense.

## Recommended collaboration model

Do not ask one employer to control the site, worker recruitment, methodology, analysis, and publication. The strongest pilot is a small consortium:

1. **Site host:** one construction project or one plantation estate.
2. **Independent research and OSH partner:** responsible for protocol, reference measurements, ethics route, and analysis.
3. **Worker voice:** the site's worker representatives, safety committee, relevant union, or an independent worker observer.
4. **CoreSense:** devices, dashboard, training, technical support, data-quality reporting, and a traceable evidence export.

Existing heat controls remain active throughout the study. CoreSense starts in shadow mode and does not replace the host's risk assessment, competent persons, emergency response, or legal duties.

## First outreach wave

### 1. Sunway iLabs with Sunway Construction - best published corporate pilot pathway

**Why it fits:** Sunway iLabs explicitly supports pilot deployments and real-world validation inside Sunway's living-lab ecosystem. Sunway Construction provides the relevant workforce and site context.

**Value to them:** A governed worker-wellbeing and smart-construction study; an auditable heat-control evidence layer; real feedback on usability, supervisor workload, and deployment readiness.

**First ask:** Route a 30-minute scoping session to Sunway Construction's QESH/HSE team. Propose one project zone, approximately 20-30 consenting workers, two weeks of shadow data, and jointly agreed gates before any active advisory use.

**Official route:** [Sunway iLabs partnership page](https://innovationlabs.sunway.edu.my/about-us/) and [Sunway Construction contact](https://www.sunwayconstruction.com.my/contact-us).

### 2. CREAM / CIDB Malaysia - construction research and industry convening

**Why it fits:** CREAM is CIDB's construction R&D body, coordinates industry research, and operates SHASSIC-related assessment activities. It has published examples of formal industry research agreements.

**Value to them:** A practical digital-OSH research case, a reusable heat-risk evidence template, and findings that could support industry knowledge transfer.

**First ask:** Co-design the construction protocol, nominate a SHASSIC-domain reviewer, and help identify one voluntary contractor/site. Do not request or imply CIDB certification or endorsement.

**Official route:** [CREAM](https://www.cream.my/home), [SHASSIC assessment information](https://www.cream.my/penilaian), and [CREAM collaboration example](https://www.cidb.gov.my/eng/cream-and-ace-greencemt-partner/).

### 3. IOI Plantation with Universiti Putra Malaysia - strongest palm-oil pilot stack

**Why it fits:** IOI reports estate-wide high-speed connectivity across more than 100 plantation estates, existing OSH innovation, and a 2025 research partnership with UPM's Institute of Plantation Studies. UPM can add independent occupational-health methodology and an institutional ethics route.

**Value to them:** Evidence on field heat exposure, signal reliability, response feasibility, and connectivity-supported intervention workflows, with independent analysis and a controlled ESG/OSH evidence pack.

**First ask:** One connected estate, several representative job roles, approximately 20-30 consenting workers, and a 6-10 week shadow-first study co-designed with UPM. Employer dashboards receive aggregate operational information; individual physiological histories are not used for discipline or productivity scoring.

**Official route:** [IOI connected-estate programme](https://www.ioigroup.com/media-releases/ioi-accelerates-digital-transformation-across-our-plantations-with-estate-wide-high-speed-connectivity/), [IOI contact channels](https://www.ioigroup.com/contact-us/), [UPM Environmental and Occupational Health](https://medic.upm.edu.my/jabatan/jabatan_kesihatan_persekitaran_dan_pekerjaan/pengenalan-55), and [UPM Institute of Plantation Studies](https://ikp.upm.edu.my/about_us/corporate_information/introduction-57775).

### 4. NIOSH Malaysia - independent method, comparator, and training partner

**Why it fits:** NIOSH Malaysia's Research Management Centre facilitates collaborative OSH research, and NIOSH offers research, consultation, laboratory, and training services.

**Value to them:** A practical Malaysian occupational-heat evaluation case and a pathway from evidence to worker/supervisor training.

**First ask:** A scoped protocol and reference-measurement review covering WBGT comparator selection, calibration checks, data-quality thresholds, consent, worker training, and publication boundaries. This is not a request for product certification.

**Official route:** [NIOSH Research and Development](https://www.niosh.com.my/26-core-activities?start=16) and [NIOSH headquarters](https://www.niosh.com.my/contact-us/headquarters).

### 5. Gamuda Engineering - advanced digital construction host

**Why it fits:** Gamuda publishes an established OSH management approach and digital construction-safety workflows, making it a strong operational-integration candidate.

**Value to them:** A worker-level heat-risk evidence layer that could be assessed alongside existing site safety data, controls, and review cycles.

**First ask:** One project, one defined microclimate zone, a shadow-first cohort, and sponsorship by both site SHE and sustainability. Phase one should avoid systems integration.

**Official route:** [Gamuda quality, safety, health and environment](https://gamuda.com/our-expertise/quality-safety-health-environment/), [digital construction safety](https://gamuda.com/2025/07/digitalising-construction-safety/news/), and [Gamuda contact](https://gamuda.com/contact-us/).

## Standards advisers, not pilot endorsers

- **DOSH/JKKP:** approach only after the protocol is credible, for a technical-scoping discussion on WBGT, calibration, competent-person alignment, and the DOSH heat-stress guideline. Do not ask DOSH to act as a commercial site host or describe its advice as product approval. [DOSH industrial-hygiene guidance](https://dosh.gov.my/en/perundangan/garis-panduan/higien-industri-garis-panduan/)
- **MSPO:** request review of how the evidence pack could attach to relevant audit controls. Do not imply that CoreSense certifies MSPO conformity. [MSPO standards](https://mspo.org.my/standards/)

## Funding and commercialization enablers

- **Cradle Fund:** current published programmes include an MVP-stage conditional grant up to RM150,000 and a commercialization-stage conditional convertible grant up to RM600,000, subject to eligibility and current programme terms. [Cradle grants](https://www.cradle.com.my/grants/)
- **MRANTI:** useful for commercialization coaching, prototyping connections, and industry introductions; current programme availability must be confirmed before relying on it. [MRANTI R2C](https://mranti.my/innovators/learn/r2c-bootcamps)

## Non-negotiable participant protections

- Independent or appropriately governed ethics review before collecting worker physiological or health-related data.
- Individual, multilingual informed consent and withdrawal without employment consequences.
- No recruitment pressure from direct supervisors; include worker representatives in protocol and materials review.
- Pseudonymous worker codes; no NRIC, passport, personal phone, raw PPG, or unnecessary precise location.
- No use for discipline, pay, attendance, productivity scoring, immigration decisions, or medical diagnosis.
- Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information. Access to individual histories is limited to the approved safety/research role and documented purpose.
- Existing heat controls, emergency procedures, competent-person decisions, and stop-work rights remain in force.
- Pre-agreed publication and negative-result rights, conflict disclosure, retention period, incident procedure, and data deletion/export terms.

These controls align with Malaysia's published personal-data principles on consent, notice and choice, disclosure, security, retention, integrity, and access. See the [Personal Data Protection Department](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/principles-of-personal-data-protection/).

## Recommended outreach sequence

1. Ask **UPM and NIOSH** for a protocol-scoping call.
2. Ask **Sunway iLabs/SunCon, Gamuda, and IOI** in parallel for a site-sponsor discussion.
3. Ask **CREAM or the relevant industry association** to help recruit a second site only after the study scope is defensible.
4. Add a **worker representative** before participant materials and consent are finalized.
5. Select the first site only when it has a named sponsor, consent feasibility, calibrated comparator access, worker consultation, and a written data-use boundary.
6. Run shadow mode before any active advisory phase, then publish only reviewed, de-identified findings.
