## Proposed subject

CoreSense proposal: shadow-first Malaysian worker heat-risk feasibility pilot

## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear Worker-representation and occupational-safety team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. Worker-side review can strengthen participant trust, multilingual consent, non-retaliation controls, grievance access, and the fairness of study recruitment.

We propose review participant information and consent materials and nominate an independent worker observer; do not recruit workers under employer pressure. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide MTUC or the relevant worker-representation channel with:

- A formal voice in worker-facing technology safeguards
- Transparent review of consent, grievance, and prohibited-use terms
- Access to anonymized findings and the worker debrief

CoreSense would receive:

- Independent feedback on trust and comprehension
- Stronger non-retaliation and grievance safeguards
- A more defensible and ethical recruitment process

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
