## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear NIOSH Research Management team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. NIOSH Malaysia publishes collaborative occupational-safety research, consultation, laboratory, and training capabilities.

We propose a method-scoping call covering WBGT reference measurement, calibration checks, data quality, training, consent, and publication boundaries; no product certification is requested. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide NIOSH Malaysia with:

- A practical Malaysian occupational-heat evaluation case
- Evidence that can inform worker and supervisor training
- A transparent study with pre-agreed negative-result rights

CoreSense would receive:

- Independent comparator and calibration advice
- Critical review of the protocol and success gates
- A credible training and research pathway

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
