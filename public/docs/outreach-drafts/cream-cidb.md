## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear CREAM research team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. CREAM is CIDB's construction research body and publishes construction R&D, collaboration, and SHASSIC-related activity.

We propose a protocol-review call and help identifying one voluntary construction site; no certification or endorsement is requested. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide CREAM / CIDB Malaysia with:

- A practical digital-OSH research case
- A reusable heat-risk evidence template for industry learning
- A bounded knowledge-transfer opportunity with documented limitations

CoreSense would receive:

- Independent protocol critique
- Construction-sector context for the evidence model
- A route to a voluntary second site after the method is defensible

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
