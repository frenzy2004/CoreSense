## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear Gamuda SHE and sustainability team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. Gamuda publishes mature safety-management and digital construction-safety workflows that can support an operational-integration study.

We propose a 30-minute discussion about one project, one defined microclimate zone, and a shadow-first cohort sponsored jointly by site SHE and sustainability. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide Gamuda Engineering with:

- A worker-level heat-risk evidence layer assessed alongside existing controls
- Operational findings on alert workload and response traceability
- A bounded pilot with no phase-one systems integration

CoreSense would receive:

- Feedback from a digitally mature construction operation
- Evidence about integration and supervisor workload
- A second construction-host option

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
