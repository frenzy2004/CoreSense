## Proposed subject

CoreSense proposal: shadow-first Malaysian worker heat-risk feasibility pilot

## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear Sunway iLabs and QESH team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. Sunway iLabs publishes a pilot-deployment and real-world-validation pathway, while Sunway Construction provides an appropriate operating context.

We propose a 30-minute scoping call about one controlled project zone, approximately 20-30 consenting adult workers, and a shadow-first feasibility study. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide Sunway iLabs and Sunway Construction with:

- A governed worker-wellbeing and smart-construction feasibility study
- An anonymized heat-risk and control-response findings pack
- Usability, supervisor-workload, and deployment-readiness findings

CoreSense would receive:

- Real construction-site evidence on device and workflow reliability
- Independent QESH feedback on the dashboard and evidence trail
- A defensible go, revise, repeat, or stop decision

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
