CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. IJM publishes occupational-safety management and digital-site monitoring activity suitable for a shadow-mode pilot discussion.

We propose route a one-project shadow-pilot discussion to Construction HSE and Digital Transformation. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide IJM Construction with:

- A low-disruption assessment of worker heat-risk evidence
- Site-specific response and usability findings
- A corrective-action briefing without compliance or certification claims

CoreSense would receive:

- A construction replication site
- Feedback on digital-site workflow fit
- Evidence on portability across projects

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

CoreSense project team
