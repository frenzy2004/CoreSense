## Proposed subject

CoreSense proposal: shadow-first Malaysian worker heat-risk feasibility pilot

## Message to send only after explicit approval

Dear IOI sustainability team and UPM research team,

CoreSense is seeking collaborators for a bounded Malaysian field study of a worker heat-risk advisory system. IOI publishes connected-estate and occupational-safety innovation activity, and UPM provides plantation and occupational-health research capabilities and an ethics route.

We propose a joint scoping call for one connected estate, representative work roles, approximately 20-30 consenting adult workers, and a 6-10 week shadow-first study. The study would begin in shadow mode, keep every existing heat control and emergency procedure in force, and use independently reviewed success gates before any active advisory phase.

The collaboration could provide IOI Plantation and Universiti Putra Malaysia with:

- Evidence on field heat exposure, signal quality, and intervention feasibility
- Independent academic method and ethics oversight
- A controlled OSH and ESG evidence pack without employee productivity scoring

CoreSense would receive:

- Rural connectivity and charging evidence
- Plantation-specific usability and multilingual feedback
- Independent evaluation of limitations and scale readiness

The study is designed around individual, multilingual informed consent; withdrawal without employment consequences; pseudonymous worker codes; and a written prohibition on discipline, pay, attendance, immigration, productivity scoring, unrelated surveillance, or medical diagnosis. Raw PPG is not uploaded. Employer operational views use aggregate or action-oriented information, and the host retains legal, HSE, emergency, and stop-work authority.

CoreSense is an occupational screening and advisory project, not a clinical thermometer. We are not requesting certification, regulator approval, endorsement, or replacement of the partner's existing risk assessment and competent-person decisions.

Would your team be open to a 30-minute scoping discussion with the relevant HSE, research, sustainability, or innovation owner?

Kind regards,

CoreSense project team
