# CoreSense field footage and product shoot pack

**Version:** 1.0 · 9 August 2026  
**Owner:** Icy  
**Purpose:** capture genuine, consented evidence for the 7:45 competition video  
**Boundary:** staged demonstrations are labelled; AIGC is never presented as field footage; no worker is exposed to heat, rain, traffic, machinery, or a fall for a shot.

## 1. What the footage must prove

The field sequence should make one impact loop visible:

`risk appears → worker receives an action → worker acknowledges → supervisor coordinates → the event becomes reviewable evidence`

Film prevention, not injury. A safe hydration or shelter response is more credible than a dramatic collapse. The watch is a prototype safety aid, not a clinical thermometer, emergency service, or substitute for WBGT and site controls.

## 2. Minimum permission gate

Do not roll camera until all boxes are checked:

- Written site-owner or site-manager permission, including the permitted areas and time window.
- Informed appearance releases from every identifiable person; provide a no-camera route for anyone who declines.
- A site safety escort and toolbox briefing; normal PPE and operating rules remain in force.
- Approval for visible logos, uniforms, number plates, documents, dashboards, and machinery.
- No minors, patient information, worker names, phone numbers, access badges, or raw health traces in frame.
- No filming during a live warning, active lifting operation, moving-vehicle conflict, thunderstorm, or other unsafe condition.
- An agreed label for each shot: `FIELD DEMONSTRATION`, `LAB DEMONSTRATION`, or `AIGC CONCEPT FOOTAGE`.

## 4. Real product-footage setup

The existing AIGC product clip is a visual concept only. Replace it with these real shots as soon as the assembled enclosure exists:

1. **Hero rotation:** phone locked on a tripod, dark neutral background, band on a manual turntable, one slow 20-degree rotation, 10 seconds.
2. **Round display macro:** expose for the screen; show one page at a time, without flicker or blown highlights.
3. **Single-button interaction:** gloved short tap, page hold, and five-second SOS hold in separate takes.
4. **Wrist-raise wake:** profile view showing the display off, wrist raise, display on, and ten-second timeout.
5. **Sensor underside:** clean macro of the contact surface and strap; do not claim waterproofing or certification.
6. **Scale:** prototype beside a metric ruler and on a calibrated scale; call mass `NOT_MEASURED` until the reading is captured.

Recommended low-cost rig: recent phone with 4K video, tripod, two diffused LED lamps at 45 degrees, white card for fill, black cloth background, and a lint-free wipe. Disable digital stabilization when locked on the tripod. Lock focus, exposure, and white balance between takes.

## 6. Capture and edit QC

- Spoken claims match the current build and evidence register.
- Any simulated value or action is visibly marked `SIMULATION` or `DEMO_UNVALIDATED`.
- AIGC carries `AI-GENERATED CONCEPT VISUAL · NOT FIELD FOOTAGE` for its full duration.
- No AIGC worker quote, testimonial, partner logo, measured improvement, or deployment claim.
- Weather forecasts are planning context; measured WBGT remains authoritative for present heat controls.
- The MPU6050 is described only as wrist-raise and possible-fall input—not rain, flood, or earthquake detection.
- Manual SOS routes to the site supervisor/HSE workflow; it does not call 999.
- Raw PPG/IMU traces and identifiable worker data are absent from the master edit.
- Music is licensed; dialogue is intelligible on phone speakers; captions are checked by a human.
- Export master: H.264, 1920×1080, 25/30 fps, 15–25 Mb/s, AAC 48 kHz, and total runtime below 8:00.
